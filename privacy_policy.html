Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Viana Stay ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile and web applications designed for hostel management, student requests, security passes, and warden administration.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to deliver hostel administration services effectively:

  • Identity Data: Full Name, Student/Staff ID, Registration Number, Hostel Name, Floor, Wing, and Room Number.
  • Contact Data: Phone number and institutional email address.
  • Service & Leave Requests: Request details, purpose of leave, destination, arrival/departure dates, and attached proof photos or document images.
  • Attendance & Security Logs: Biometric/check-in logs, gate pass entries, and attendance records.

2. Camera & Photo Attachment Permissions

Our application requests permission to access your device’s Camera and Photo Gallery solely for user-initiated actions:

  • Capturing mandatory document photos for leave requests and gate passes.
  • Attaching photos of maintenance issues (e.g., AC repair, electrical, or plumbing defects).

We do not access or capture photos in the background.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for institutional hostel administration, including:

  • Processing leave, room allocation, maintenance, and security requests.
  • Communicating status updates and emergency notifications via FCM push notifications.
  • Verifying student presence and generating administrative hostel reports.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All communication is transmitted via secure HTTP/HTTPS endpoints with strict database access controls.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Information is shared only with authorized institutional staff (Wardens, Maintenance Personnel, Security Staff) relevant to resolving your requests.
